## TL;DR

W20's unifying thread: **BDD infrastructure grew up.** The team realized `.agent.md` files were fragile (environment-dependent, hard to run in CI) and systematically migrated to `reproduce.sh` with an IoC pattern. This unlocked plan-mode `--dry-run` coverage across arena (61 tests), cold-pool (7), curator (29), and deck (10) — a new standard for integration testing without LLM costs. Meanwhile, the arena vs mode's judge coupling was finally decoupled (pr-1/2/3), and a full IO separation audit confirmed the architecture was already clean.

## Project Lesson Candidates

- **reproduce.sh IoC > .agent.md**: Environment injection through stdin/args makes BDD runnable in CI, local, and container without file-system-dependent setup
- **Plan-mode is the integration test sweet spot**: `--dry-run` exercises real logic paths (parsing, planning, validation) without paying LLM token costs — 100+ tests added this week
- **API instability is a first-class risk for arena vs**: Two independent attempts failed at the same timeout boundary due to Anthropic errors. Shorter probe timeouts or retry logic may be needed.
