### Key cognitive insight
Classical vs Bayesian probability **language-game** in verification:
- Classical: P(A∧B) ≤ min(P(A), P(B)) — iron law
- Bayesian: P(A∧B | evidence) can genuinely be > P(A | evidence) when evidence is informative
- CLI should NOT blindly flag AND with confidence > min(premises) as fallacy
- CLI SHOULD flag AND where premises lack independent evidence (circular, author-only)
- Detective reasoning (rare condition → high suspicion) is legitimate Bayes, not fallacy
- Coinflip 10 heads: classical says (1/2)^10, Bayesian says coin is rigged
